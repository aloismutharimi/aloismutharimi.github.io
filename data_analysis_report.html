Data Analysis Report

Python · Pandas · NumPy · 28-day fitness analysis

What it does

Loads 28 days of fitness logs into a DataFrame and produces a structured weekly and monthly report.

  • Overall metrics — total steps, averages, 10K hit rate, sleep, bench press trend
  • Weekly breakdown — per-week averages and 10K+ day counts
  • Protocol comparison — OMAD vs 2MAD side-by-side
  • Top 3 step days — best performances with their protocols

Why it exists

Raw fitness logs are hard to interpret without structure. This project turns 28 days of numbers into weekly trends, protocol comparisons, and a clean report.
